FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

How does Antixorcar verify each vehicle?

Every vehicle on Antixorcar.com must pass our comprehensive 160-point multi-stage inspection conducted by certified technicians. We verify title history, structural integrity, onboard diagnostics, braking systems, and powertrain condition before issuing the Antixor Verified badge.

Can I test drive a car before committing to buy?

Yes, absolutely. You can schedule a private showroom or doorstep test drive directly from any vehicle details page. Our dealer concierges will prepare the vehicle and coordinate at your convenience.

How fast can I sell my vehicle on Antixorcar.com?

Using our 5-step selling wizard, listing takes under 3 minutes. Over 78% of vehicles priced accurately receive verified dealer purchase offers within the first 48 hours.

Are financing pre-approvals binding?

No. Pre-approval utilizes a soft credit inquiry that does not affect your credit score and involves zero obligation. You can explore APR terms and down payment plans before making your final decision.
